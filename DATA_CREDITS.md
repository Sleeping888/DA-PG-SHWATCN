# Third-party dataset credit

The predictions and target arrays here are derived from SDWPF: A Dataset for Spatial Dynamic Wind
Power Forecasting over a Large Turbine Array (Zhou et al., 2024),
https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24798654, distributed under CC BY 4.0. The raw SDWPF files are not
duplicated; obtain them from that record and cite it together with the associated Scientific Data
paper. The target arrays cover the 2021 test subset after the processing recorded in this repository.
