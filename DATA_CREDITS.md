# Third-party dataset credit

The predictions and target arrays in this repository are derived from SDWPF: A Dataset for Spatial Dynamic Wind Power Forecasting over a Large Turbine Array (Zhou et al., 2024), https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.24798654. The Figshare dataset states a CC BY 4.0 licence. The raw SDWPF files are not duplicated here; obtain them from the original record and cite that record and its associated Scientific Data paper. The target arrays represent the 2021 test subset after the exact processing recorded in this repository.
