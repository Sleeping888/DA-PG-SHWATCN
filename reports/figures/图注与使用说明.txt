英文图注：
Comparison of six-step wind-power forecasts across nine models. Panels (a–c) show turbine-level RMSE, MAE and R²; panels (d–f) show the corresponding wind-farm metrics. Lines and markers denote means over three training seeds (84, 101 and 1234), and error bars indicate sample standard deviations, not confidence intervals. Forecast lead times t+1 to t+6 correspond nominally to 10–60 min. The main figure uses only finite, nonnegative original power observations as test labels; farm-level scores require valid observations for all 134 turbines. Predictions are from the previously selected checkpoints without retraining. Original retained timestamps include gaps; the scoring mask changes evaluation only, while training and checkpoint selection retain the original protocol.
